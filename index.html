The Wanderer
I The Silence Before

Before the world
learned to breathe

There was only the hum of possibility —
a vast and breathing dark that held
the weight of everything not yet born.
The wanderer stood at the edge of all known things,
watching the universe fold into itself
like a letter written to no one, and everyone.

II The Awakening

Something stirred
beneath the still

Colors that had no names bloomed like ink in water,
spreading through the silence with the slow confidence
of something that has always been there, waiting.
The wanderer stepped forward into the becoming —
and the world opened like a palm.

III Into the Abyss

The storms
did not frighten

They clarified. Every bolt of lightning
was a word the sky had been meaning to say
for a thousand years of patient waiting.
The wanderer walked through the howling
and found that it was only wind —
and wind has no memory of its origins.

IV The Revelation

At the center
lived a stillness

More profound than silence. More resonant than sound.
The wanderer recognized it the way you recognize
a face you've never seen but always known —
as the quiet note beneath all music,
the pause that makes the song mean something.

V New Dawn

When the light
returned, unhurried

It found a different wanderer
standing in the same shoes.
The journey had not ended —
it had only just begun to understand itself.
And the world, older and new at once,
exhaled for the very first time.
